# Credits

Chronicles of Rose is an early portfolio project by Harsh, developed with AI
assistance. The scene uses procedural geometry and synthesized ambient sound.

- **Three.js:** bundled in `vendor/`, under the included
  [MIT license](vendor/LICENSE.txt).
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans are requested from Google Fonts,
  with Georgia and Arial as fallbacks. Font files are not bundled.
- **Visual inspiration:** stylized gardens and lighting inspired by Genshin
  Impact; the mouse-guided flight and glow of
  [HelloEnjoy's Lights](https://helloenjoy.itch.io/lights); environmental wind
  guidance inspired by Ghost of Tsushima.

These games' assets, music, and source code are not included.
